\section{Further Evaluation}
\label{app:evaluation-agenda}
The confirmation study supplies optimized-library baselines, repeated sessions,
inverse jobs, filtering chains, and selected state/load extensions. Its main
remaining questions concern different execution models, stronger replication,
and actual host integration, rather than another measurement of the same grid.

\paragraph{Asynchronous and overlap-reusing alternatives.}
A worker can remove analysis from the engine thread when asynchronous delivery
is acceptable, as considered by Battenberg and
Avi\v{z}ienis~\cite{battenberg2011}. A matched experiment should include capture,
queueing, missed publications, consumer contention, and actual spectrum age.
For heavily overlapping windows or a small requested bin set, hopping,
windowed sliding, and feedforward methods deserve separate comparisons
\cite{garrido2016,park2014,rafii2018,eleftheriadis2023}. Equivalent windowing,
normalization, smoothing, and output processing are essential. A periodic Hann
window permits an exact short window kernel; approximate truncated kernels
would require a separately stated accuracy target. None of the present FFT
backend results ranks these alternatives.

\paragraph{Numerical stress and applications.}
Van der Byl and Inggs distinguish stability from useful numerical accuracy in
sliding structures~\cite{vanderbyl2016}. Long streams with weak tones beside
strong ones, silence after a strong signal, and phase-sensitive processing
would extend the current deterministic fixtures. The binary64 references here
are not exact arithmetic. Non-power-of-two lengths, more windows, and
application-specific error budgets remain untested. The overlap-save identity
and three-tap FIR establish processing semantics; realistic long impulse
responses and frequency-dependent effects would be needed before recommending
FFT filtering for an application. A complete bounded-stage inverse pipeline
would also have to distribute the bulk initialization identified in
Section~\ref{sec:inverse-chain}.

\paragraph{Device and display behavior.}
For a visualizer, producer publication is only one component of perceived
latency. The decomposition of Pr\r{u}\v{s}a and Holighaus~\cite{prusa2016}
motivates measuring consumption and repaint separately. The mailbox permits
superseded snapshots when the display is slower than production. A Rack study
should measure the real callback schedule, driver-reported underruns,
concurrent displays, and patch load. Callback phase and analyzer count matter
because individually tolerable bursts can coincide, as staggered convolution
schedules also illustrate~\cite{borss2009}.

Skare separates continuous throughput from periodic callback measurements and
emphasizes their difference from plugin invocation within a
DAW~\cite{skare2024}. Balasubramaniam et al. distinguish plugin timing, session
callback utilization, driver underruns, and worker inference underruns under
controlled contention~\cite{balasubramaniam2026}. Their virtual driver does
not include physical-interface behavior. These methods inform the next host
experiment; their GPU and inference results are not FFT-backend evidence.
The present synchronous harness measures computation, not audio reliability.

\paragraph{Replication and granularity.}
The three temporal blocks provide observed variation on one host. Additional
days, builds, architectures, compilers, and operating systems should be chosen
before making general platform claims. Kalibera and Jones provide a framework
for allocating repetitions across sources of variation~\cite{kalibera2013};
layout and environment bias also require attention~\cite{mytkowicz2009}.
Longer sessions would help characterize infrequent stalls but would still not
supply a safe worst-case bound~\cite{wilhelm2008}.

The current weights distribute operation density without making operations
equal in duration. Following Wefers' discussion of transform subdivision
\cite{wefers2015}, a controlled study could vary butterfly batches or small
subtransforms under the same hop and publication constraint. The depth-first
traversal of B\'ecoulet and Verguet~\cite{becoulet2021} suggests comparing
immediate and resumable forms of the same alternative kernel across working
sets and analyzer counts. That would separate locality and factorization from
suspension. Hardware-calibrated weights might improve typical behavior, but
would add assumptions rather than prove execution time on a general-purpose OS.
