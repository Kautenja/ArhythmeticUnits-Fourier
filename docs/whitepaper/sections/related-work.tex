\section{Related Work and Positioning}
\label{sec:related}
Time-distributed FFT computation predates this implementation. Lo and Lee
schedule butterfly work during acquisition and also combine scheduling with
reuse across shifted frames~\cite{lo1998,lo1999,lomoving1999}. In convolution,
Bor\ss{} distributes partition work across processing cycles and staggers
filter schedules~\cite{borss2009}; Hurchalla distributes FFT processor and
memory load across incoming blocks on one thread~\cite{hurchalla2010}.
These are precedents for controlling bursts rather than only aggregate cost.
Our analyzer instead selects a complete window before processing it and
retains those samples while packing incrementally.

Battenberg and Avi\v{z}ienis distribute forward transforms, spectral products,
and inverse transforms across callbacks, and compare cooperative with
preemptive execution~\cite{battenberg2011}. Scheduling a whole processing
chain therefore also has precedent. Wefers discusses such manual preemption
and FFT subdivision more broadly~\cite{wefers2015}. The present contribution
is the analysis and implementation of an exact-hop windowed-analysis sequence,
including retained-input capacity, cancellation, and output ownership. It
claims neither a new FFT factorization nor the invention of time distribution.

Pr\r{u}\v{s}a and Holighaus provide a worker-thread alternative for spectral
visualization and distinguish analysis delay from polling and repaint
delay~\cite{prusa2016}. Sliding, hopping, and feedforward methods instead reuse
work across windows~\cite{lyons2021,park2014,garrido2016}; frequency-domain
window kernels can make that reuse compatible with tapered
windows~\cite{rafii2018,eleftheriadis2023}. Our scheduler starts a fresh
transform for each selected frame. Comparing these alternatives requires
matching the requested bins, hop, window, normalization, and numerical behavior;
lower aggregate arithmetic alone does not establish smaller processing bursts.

FFTW, Rack's PFFFT wrapper, and Apple Accelerate/vDSP are relevant optimized
batch baselines~\cite{frigo2005,pffft,vdsp}. The original scalar FFT campaign holds
the transform implementation fixed to examine work placement. Neither archived
campaign measures those libraries or establishes a performance ranking
against them. Appendix~\ref{app:related-work} preserves the full literature
comparison, including transform foundations, overlap reuse, traversal choices,
and library-specific matching requirements.
