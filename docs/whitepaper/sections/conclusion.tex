\section{Conclusion}
A complete windowed analyzer can be expressed as bounded,
dependency-ordered operations and advanced over an exact publication hop.
The resulting contract accounts for work credits, retained input,
cancellation, and ownership of complete spectra. It addresses the surrounding
preparation and output stages that remain concentrated when only FFT
butterflies are resumable.

The confirmation study establishes a practical tradeoff on the measured M1 Pro
host: cooperative analysis can reduce callback p99 at the expense of total
cost and spectrum age. Matched controls distinguish that placement effect
from the benefits of optimized kernels, and the native PFFFT hybrid provides
an intermediate option. Periodic inverse jobs and overlap-save chains retain
bulk stages and include cases favoring native batch processing; their results
do not inherit the complete analyzer's bound. The value of suspension is
therefore conditional on the full processing path and the caller's priorities.
